Sign in to your online account to check how much Simple Assessment tax you need to pay.

There is no other way to find out how much you owe, so we advise checking as soon as possible.

Checking early will help you plan ahead and avoid last minute stress.

What you need to do now:

  1. Go to GOV.UK and search ‘online account’

  2. Sign in

  3. Select your PAYE account

  4. Check your tax bill and messages


You can also do this in the free HMRC app. Because you’ve chosen to receive tax letters online, we’ll e-mail you when there’s something waiting for you in your online account.
